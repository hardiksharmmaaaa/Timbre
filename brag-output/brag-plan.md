# Brag Plan: Timbre

## What is this app?
Timbre is a voice-emotion-aware companion prototype that turns pitch, rhythm, energy, and texture into an uncertainty-aware vocal-affect signal a conversational AI can use.

## Planning rubric
1. **What is the app?** A research prototype that adds vocal context to digital conversation while keeping uncertainty visible.
2. **Most impressive claim:** “Timbre returns a distribution, not a confident label about a person.”
3. **Visual hook:** The black-and-warm-white interface, orange signal glow, animated waveform, and probability ring.
4. **Actual UI to show:** The live-demo product shell: Ready to listen → live vocal input → current signal and conversational response.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 seconds; this leaves enough time to show one complete interaction and hold the safety boundary legibly.
6. **Tone:** polished; clean, professional product demo with quiet research confidence.
7. **Audio feel:** A steady, low-volume professional music bed with three restrained, motion-matched accents.
8. **Share caption:** Introducing Timbre: vocal context for conversational AI, with uncertainty kept visible.
9. **User flow:** Tap to begin → a two-second voice window becomes a labeled probability signal → the companion adapts its response without claiming to know the speaker’s internal state.

## The angle
Make invisible vocal context feel tangible without overstating what the model knows. The video moves from a simple sentence to an inspectable signal and then to a more considered response, with uncertainty and the research boundary always visible.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On Timbre’s near-black field, an orange waveform draws into view as the product’s real headline resolves: “Voice carries tone.” The word “tone.” lands in orange while the glow breathes with the music.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Recreate the product shell’s “Ready to listen” state and simulate one deliberate click on the circular start control.
- Transform the shell into the live dashboard: a two-second window, “calm” as the strongest demo pattern, a 64% probability ring, a visible ±5.8% interval, and a small distribution of alternatives.
- Reveal a companion reply—“We can take this one step at a time. What’s next?”—beside an explicit “SIMULATED DEMO · MOCK INFERENCE” label.

## Outro / punchline
Land on the real safety line, “A voice is a signal, not a confession.” Then resolve to the Timbre wordmark and “Voice, in context.”

## User flow worth showing
Tap to begin → inspect an uncertainty-aware vocal-affect estimate → receive a more considered conversational response.

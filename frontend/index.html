01 / Context

The words stay the same.
The signal moves.

“I’m fine” can sound steady, hesitant, bright, or strained. Timbre preserves that extra layer for a conversational system—without treating it as inner truth.

04 / Safety

A voice is a signal,
not a confession.

Timbre estimates patterns in how a voice sounds. It does not know how someone truly feels, and ambiguity remains visible in the output.

06 / Research integrity

Measured honestly.
Built in the open.

Data

Speaker-independent splits and cached features protect the evaluation boundary.

Models

Classical acoustic pipeline and hand-written Viterbi remain distinct from LLM glue.

Evaluation

Accuracy, balanced accuracy, calibration, ablations, and cross-dataset tests are reported from reproducible runs.

Limitations

Acted English datasets and cross-lingual degradation are documented, not hidden.
